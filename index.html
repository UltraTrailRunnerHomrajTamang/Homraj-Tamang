A little about me

The mountains
are calling.

Namaste! I am Homraj Tamang, a passionate trail runner from the beautiful mountains of Nepal. Growing up surrounded by the Himalayas, I have always felt a deep connection to the trails.

Running through high-altitude paths, steep ascents, and remote villages is not just a sport for me; it is a way of life. Whether racing ultra-marathons or exploring new routes in the shadow of the world's tallest peaks, I find joy and freedom in every step.

As a proud representative of Nepal's trail running community, I am always excited to share our unique landscapes and endurance culture with the world.
